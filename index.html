U.S. Weather Stations, 2017

Each dot represents a weather station, summarized over January to September 2017. Dot colors indicate average daily high temperature, with darker red representing hotter temperatures. Drag a box on either chart or use the preset buttons to jump to the hottest, coldest, or wettest 10% of stations. Selected stations are highlighted in both charts. Hover over a dot to view station details and highlight the matching station in the other chart. Click the clear selection button or empty space to reset your selection. Toggle Pan & Zoom to explore the map, and Reset view to return to the original map.

Explore the presets to discover geographic patterns: the hottest stations cluster in the South and Hawaii, while the coldest follow the Rocky Mountains and Alaska. The wettest stations appear in two distinct regions: the Southeast and the Northwest.
